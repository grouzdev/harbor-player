Features

Curate your collection

Bring your favorite music together in a personal local collection — free from streaming, subscriptions, and someone else’s catalog.

Explore your music

Connect any folders and bring scattered music together in one place. Browse folders, genres, artists, and albums.

Put things in order

Edit tags for multiple tracks at once and find missing information and cover art online.
